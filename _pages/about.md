## Background

I am a wireless software architect and I have served for 25 years in industry in
(i) Research, Development, Testing, and integration of cellular protocol stack software products 
(3G/4G/5G technologies), and (ii) Portfolio building, team building and delivery of protoctol stack
software services and solutions.  Details about my contributions in the above areas which 
I consider as my core area of expertise and the 1st innings of my career are 
available on my LinkedIn page.

Going forward, I aspire to continue contributing in my core areas as a
researcher & consultant for topics and works which I feel satisfy my
urge to learn or fulfill a pressing industry need. However, I increasingly aspire to 
 build a new career to give back to society by contributing as a consultant
in environmental and social impact initiatives driven by government and
industry. My interests in my 2nd innings as a consultant are as below and my 
qualification and works are captured in subsequent pages.

## Interests

**Core area**:-
    Create artifacts like trainings, tools, reference designs etc. based on prior experience 
    to enable teams to design and develop cellular protocol stacks;
    Provide support to businesses for project, people, product and services management;
    Provide consulting to analyse and predict trends in the telecom landscape;
    Perform research to create content (paper, ideas, opinion, policy etc.);

**Environment/Social areas**:-
    Analyse and report environmental, social and governance (ESG) problems prevalent in India;
    Analyse or propose policy solutions in ESG;
    Propose, plan and execute ESG projects for Government & Industry;
    Be a social and environmental champion to bring about needed change;
