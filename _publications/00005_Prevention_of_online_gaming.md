excerpt: 'The “Prevention and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025” regulates the online gaming sector to prevent societal harm caused due to production & consumption of inappropriate, unsafe and illegal online games. An analysis shows that the Act causes sudden disruption in the existing unregulated free market for online games and burdens the government in its new game certifying role with subjective qualifiers. This brief suggests improving the Act by (i) Reducing Government interventions in promoting any particular genre (ii) Reducing overheads due to subjectivity in approval process by letting the industry instead work in a leadership role on global standardization of device platforms and apps that would ensure safer and legal apps globally for user consumption and (iii) Slowing down and taking a phased approach by temporarily relying on self-declarations by industry for certifications until the global standards are ready within a reasonable time frame.'
