excerpt: 'The creation of India required unifying the languages of India to effectively and efficiently conduct the businesses of the Governments/Institutions of the union, states, and territories of India. This language unification exercise rolled out by our constitution and pursued by the governments for several years has resulted in an official languages status quo within governments, however the language stress persists within society. This paper tries to analyse the causes for language stress and provides an approach to relieve the stress by redefining the language problem and making changes in governments approach to solve the problem'
